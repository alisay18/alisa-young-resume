02 / Playbooks

Systems, not one-off campaigns.

Most of what I ship is plumbing: lists feed sequences, events feed nurture, and every signal lands where the team can act on it. Here are four of those systems.

A cold outbound engine that actually gets answers

The goal
Put Upscale AI in front of the right buyers every week, at volume, with messages they actually answer.
What I built
An end-to-end outbound machine: ICP lists sourced and enriched in Clay, Apollo, and Storeleads, sequenced through Instantly, with Trigify catching social signals to flag warm prospects. I own qualification from MQL through SQL handoff to sales.
The result
7k+ emails a week at a 4% reply rate, against a 1–2% benchmark.
  1. Clay · Apollo · Storeleads40+ ICP lists
  2. TrigifySocial signals flag warm leads
  3. Instantly7k+ sends / week
  4. Qualification4% reply → MQL
  5. SalesSQL handoff

From LinkedIn URL to CRM record, automatically

The problem
Sales was guessing emails from LinkedIn URLs by hand. It was slow and error-prone, and it bottlenecked the middle of the funnel.
What I built
A webhook flow: paste a LinkedIn URL, Clay enriches the contact, Slack announces it, and Zapier syncs it into HubSpot.
The result
A manual lookup became a near-instant, notification-driven flow the whole team can see and trust.

Events that end in discovery calls

The goal
Meet ICP buyers in person and turn the room into pipeline, not just a nice night.
What I built
I produced, planned, and photographed 18 events in NYC, LA, and Austin: intimate dinners, yoga activations, and happy hours with 100+ RSVPs. Promotion ran through email, Luma, and the newsletters. An interest capture system tracks everyone who couldn’t make it and nurtures them for the next one.
The result
22 ICP discovery calls, plus a warm list that grows even from no-shows.
  1. Email · Luma · NewsletterMultichannel promotion
  2. 18 eventsNYC · LA · Austin
  3. Interest captureCouldn’t attend? Tracked
  4. NurtureKept warm for next time
  5. Pipeline22 discovery calls

An owned audience that compounds

The goal
Build channels the company owns, so reach isn’t rented week to week.
What I built
Two weekly newsletters, including the automated “AI Brand Brief,” plus ICP-targeted LinkedIn content and 3+ Lovable landing pages for demand gen. Form submissions ping Slack, and HubSpot + Zapier tie campaigns to deal stages.
The result
20k subscribers at 43% and 36% open rates, and LinkedIn up 299% to 3,675 followers with 369k+ impressions.
