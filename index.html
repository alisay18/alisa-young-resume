II. Selected work

01

The outbound engine

Upscale AI

Goal

Reach the right buyers every week, at volume, with messages they actually answer.

What I built

ICP lists sourced in Clay, Apollo, and Storeleads, sequenced through Instantly, with Trigify flagging prospects who are already warm on social. I own qualification from MQL through SQL handoff.

Result

7,000+ emails a week at a 4% reply rate, two to four times the benchmark.

Clay, Apollo, Storeleads → Trigify → Instantly → MQL → SQL → Sales

02

LinkedIn URL to CRM, automatically

Upscale AI

Problem

Sales was guessing emails from LinkedIn URLs by hand. It was slow and error-prone, and it bottlenecked the middle of the funnel.

What I built

A webhook flow: submit a URL, Clay enriches the contact, Slack announces it, and Zapier syncs the record into HubSpot.

Result

A manual lookup became a near-instant flow the whole team can see and trust.

Google Sheets → Clay → Slack → Sheets → Zapier → HubSpot

03

Events that end in discovery calls

Upscale AI

Goal

Meet buyers in person and turn the room into pipeline.

What I built

Eighteen events I produced, planned, and photographed: intimate dinners, yoga activations, and happy hours with 100+ RSVPs, promoted through email, Luma, and the newsletters. An interest capture system tracks and nurtures everyone who couldn’t make it.

Result

22 ICP discovery calls, and a warm list that grows even from no-shows.

Email, Luma, newsletter → Event → Interest capture → Nurture → Discovery call

04

An audience the company owns

Upscale AI

Goal

Build channels that compound, so reach isn’t rented week to week.

What I built

Two weekly newsletters, including the automated “AI Brand Brief,” ICP-targeted LinkedIn content, and campaign landing pages in Lovable. Inbound forms ping Slack, and HubSpot ties campaigns to deal stages.

Result

20,000 subscribers opening at 43% and 36%. LinkedIn up 299%.
